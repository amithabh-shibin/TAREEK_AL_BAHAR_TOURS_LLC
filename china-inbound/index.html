FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a visa to visit China?

Most visitors need a visa, though some nationalities qualify for visa-free or transit exemptions. We check the latest rules for your passport and guide you through the process.

Which is the best time to visit?

Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer the most comfortable weather for most regions.

Are guides available in Arabic?

We arrange English-speaking guides as standard, and Arabic-speaking guides in major cities on request.

Can you plan a private tour for my family?

Yes — all itineraries can be made private and tailored to your pace and interests.
