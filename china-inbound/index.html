Transit made clear

Choose the right transit option

Both options require onward travel to a third country or region. Your nationality, travel document and route determine eligibility.

Up to 24 hours

Short international transit

All open exit-entry ports in China implement the 24-hour visa-free transit policy for nationals of all other countries around the world. Foreigners transiting through China to a third country or region may stay in China for no more than 24 hours without a visa, provided that they hold valid international travel documents and onward international flight/ship/train tickets with confirmed seats, and will not leave the restricted areas of ports. Any foreigner who does need to leave the designated restricted area must first obtain a temporary entry permit at the immigration inspection authority of the port concerned.

Leaving the port’s restricted area requires a temporary entry permit from immigration inspection.

Up to 240 hours · 10 days

Explore between connections

China currently applies the 240-hour visa-free transit policy to nationals of 57 countries, including Russia, the United Kingdom and the United States. Nationals of these countries, who need to transit through China to third countries or regions, may enter visa-free through any of the 65 designated open ports in 24 provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government, and stay in the permitted areas for no more than 10 days, provided that they hold ordinary passports and onward tickets with confirmed seats and departure dates. Within the designated areas, they may engage in activities such as tourism, business, visits and family reunions. Activities such as work, study or news reporting still require prior approvals and appropriate visas.

Entry ports & permitted travel areas

Ports and permitted stay areas: The 65 open ports cover major cities including Beijing (Beijing Capital International Airport, Beijing Daxing International Airport), Shanghai (Shanghai Pudong International Airport, Shanghai Hongqiao Port, etc.), Tianjin, Guangzhou, Shenzhen, Chengdu and Xi'an. In Guangdong Province, the permitted stay area covers the whole province, and travellers may exit from any open port in the province. In Guangxi, the permitted stay area covers 14 cities: Nanning, Liuzhou, Guilin, Wuzhou, Beihai, Fangchenggang, Qinzhou, Guigang, Yulin, Baise, Hezhou, Hechi, Laibin and Chongzuo.

Six regional visa-free entry schemes

From Hainan stays to organized city and cruise tours, each scheme has its own passport, travel-agency, entry-port and geographic requirements.

Regional stay periods below are calculated from 00:00 on the day after entry. Separate bilateral or unilateral exemptions follow their own rules.

Up to 30 days

Hainan Province

61 eligible nationalities · Ordinary passports

Eligibility, ports & permitted areas

Foreign nationals holding ordinary passports from 61 countries may enter and stay within the administrative region of Hainan Province for a maximum of 30 days without a visa, for short-term stays associated with tourism, business, visits, family visits, medical treatment, conferences and exhibitions, sports competitions, etc. The policy does not apply to those who intend to enter for working or studying. The exit-entry ports include all the open ports in Hainan.

Up to 15 days

Cruise ship groups

Two or more travellers · China-registered agency

Eligibility, ports & permitted areas

Any foreign tourist group consisting of two or more individuals, which is organized and received by a travel agency registered in China, may enter China without a visa by cruise ship via one of the cruise ports in 13 cities, including Tianjin, Dalian, Shanghai, Lianyungang, Wenzhou, Zhoushan, Xiamen, Qingdao, Guangzhou, Shenzhen, Beihai, Haikou and Sanya. Eligible groups shall continue their journey to the following scheduled ports and depart China with the same cruise ship. They may stay in Beijing and 11 coastal provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government for a maximum of 15 days.

Up to 6 days

Xishuangbanna, Yunnan

ASEAN tourist groups · Group entry and exit

Eligibility, ports & permitted areas

Any tourist group (two or more individuals holding ordinary passports) from an ASEAN member state may enter and exit China through Xishuangbanna Gasa International Airport, Mohan Railway Port and Mohan Highway Port visa-free, provided that the group is organized and received by a travel agency registered in China. The group shall enter and leave China as a whole, and may stay within the administrative region of Xishuangbanna Dai Autonomous Prefecture for a maximum of 6 days. The ASEAN member states include Malaysia, Indonesia, Thailand, the Philippines, Singapore, Brunei, Vietnam, Laos, Myanmar and Cambodia.

Up to 6 days

Guilin, Guangxi

ASEAN tourist groups · Guilin airport

Eligibility, ports & permitted areas

Tourist groups from the same ASEAN member states (two or more individuals holding ordinary passports) may enter and exit China through the airport in Guilin visa-free, provided that they are organized and received by a travel agency registered in China and enter and leave China as a whole. They may stay within the administrative region of Guilin for a maximum of 6 days.

Up to 6 days

Hong Kong / Macao to Guangdong

Organized tourist groups · 10 permitted cities

Eligibility, ports & permitted areas

Foreign nationals holding ordinary passports from countries with diplomatic relations with China may enter nine cities in the Guangdong-Hong Kong-Macao Greater Bay Area, as well as Shantou, without a visa, provided that they first enter Hong Kong or Macao SAR and then enter Guangdong Province as part of a tourist group (two or more individuals) organized by a travel agency registered in either SAR. The group shall enter and exit the Chinese mainland as a whole via any of the exit-entry ports in these 10 cities, and may stay for a maximum of 6 days in Guangzhou, Foshan, Zhaoqing, Shenzhen, Dongguan, Huizhou, Zhuhai, Zhongshan, Jiangmen and Shantou.

Up to 6 days

Hong Kong / Macao to Hainan

Organized tourist groups · Hainan only

Eligibility, ports & permitted areas

Foreign nationals holding ordinary passports from countries with diplomatic relations with China may enter Hainan Province without a visa, provided that they first enter Hong Kong or Macao SAR and then enter Hainan Province as part of a tourist group (two or more individuals) organized by a travel agency registered in either SAR. The group shall enter and exit Hainan Province as a whole via any of the exit-entry ports in Hainan, and may stay for a maximum of 6 days.

Your questions, answered

Visa-free travel FAQs

What are the principal components of China's regional visa-free entry policy for foreign nationals?

The policy consists of six components: (I) the 30-day visa-free entry policy for foreign nationals entering Hainan Province; (II) the visa-free entry policy for foreign tourist groups traveling by cruise ships; (III) the visa-free entry policy for tourist groups entering Xishuangbanna, Yunnan from ASEAN member states; (IV) the visa-free entry policy for tourist groups entering Guilin, Guangxi from ASEAN member states; (V) the visa-free entry policy for foreign tourist groups traveling from Hong Kong and Macao SARs to Guangdong Province; and (VI) the visa-free entry policy for foreign tourist groups traveling from Hong Kong and Macao SARs to Hainan Province.

Which countries' nationals are eligible for the regional visa-free policy for foreigners to enter Hainan? What activities can they undertake? How long can they stay?

Foreign nationals holding ordinary passports from 61 countries—including Russia, the United Kingdom, the United States, Canada, Australia, New Zealand, the Republic of Korea, Japan, Singapore, Malaysia, Thailand, Kazakhstan, the United Arab Emirates, Brunei, Qatar, the Philippines, Indonesia, Vietnam and Kyrgyzstan—may enter Hainan without a visa for short-term stays for tourism, business, visits, family visits, medical treatment, conferences and exhibitions, sports competitions, etc. (working and studying are excluded). They may stay within the administrative region of Hainan for a maximum of 30 days, and may enter and exit through any of the open ports in Hainan.

My friend and I plan to travel to China by cruise ship. Can we enter visa-free under the policy? In which areas can we stay?

Any foreign tourist group of two or more individuals, organized and received by a travel agency registered in China, may enter visa-free by cruise ship via one of the cruise ports in 13 cities, including Tianjin, Dalian, Shanghai, Lianyungang, Wenzhou, Zhoushan, Xiamen, Qingdao, Guangzhou, Shenzhen, Beihai, Haikou and Sanya. The group shall continue to the following scheduled ports with the same cruise ship until it departs China. They may stay in Beijing and 11 coastal provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities directly under the Central Government for a maximum of 15 days.

Can tourist groups from ASEAN countries enter Xishuangbanna in Yunnan visa-free? Where else can they go?

Yes. Tourist groups of two or more individuals from ASEAN member states, holding ordinary passports and organized and received by a travel agency registered in China, may enter and exit visa-free through Xishuangbanna Gasa International Airport, Mohan Railway Port and Mohan Highway Port, and stay within Xishuangbanna Dai Autonomous Prefecture for a maximum of 6 days. In addition, ASEAN tourist groups may also enter Guilin, Guangxi visa-free through the airport in Guilin and stay within Guilin for a maximum of 6 days, under the same group-based arrangement.

After arriving in Hong Kong or Macao SAR, can foreign tourist groups enter Guangdong or Hainan visa-free?

Yes. Foreign nationals holding ordinary passports from countries with diplomatic relations with China may, after entering Hong Kong or Macao SAR, join a tourist group (two or more individuals) organized by a travel agency registered in either SAR and enter Guangdong Province visa-free—covering the nine cities in the Greater Bay Area and Shantou, with a maximum stay of 6 days. They may also enter Hainan Province visa-free with a maximum stay of 6 days. The exit-entry ports are the open ports of the 10 cities in Guangdong and all the open ports in Hainan, respectively.

How is the stay duration calculated?

Under all of the aforementioned policies, the stay period is calculated from 0:00 on the day after the day of entry. For individuals who meet the requirements of bilateral visa exemption agreements or other unilateral visa exemption policies of China, the relevant regulations shall prevail.

Information checked on 29 October 2026. Eligibility depends on your passport and itinerary; confirm the applicable policy before booking. UAE residence alone does not establish visa-free eligibility.
