Who We Are

TAREEK AL BAHAR TOURS LLC

A licensed travel and services company based in Dubai, United Arab Emirates.

We started with a simple idea: travel between the Gulf and China should be easy, safe and fairly priced. Today we welcome visitors to Dubai, send residents across the world, guide patients to trusted hospitals in China and help entrepreneurs start companies in the UAE.

Behind every booking is a real person who answers your calls, checks every document and stays available until you are back home.

  • China medical travel coordination

  • UAE business set up support
