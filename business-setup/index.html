FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does company formation take?

Free zone licences can often be issued in a few working days once documents are ready, while mainland companies typically take longer due to additional approvals.

Can I own 100% of my company?

In most activities and free zones, yes. Some regulated activities may require a local partner or approvals — we will confirm for your case.

Do I need to live in the UAE?

Not always. Requirements depend on the licence and visa type; we advise you on the best route.

Can you help with an office and a bank account?

Yes. We can arrange office space or flexi-desks and support the bank account application with our banking partners.
