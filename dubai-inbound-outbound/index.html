Dubai & Abu Dhabi Holiday

A complete holiday combining city sights, a Marina dinner cruise, desert adventures and Abu Dhabi.

View itinerary & booking details
Day 1
ARRIVAL IN DUBAI & MARINA DHOW CRUISE

Overnight Stay: Dubai

Welcome to Dubai, a remarkable destination where modern luxury meets traditional Arabian hospitality. Upon arrival at Dubai International Airport, you will be greeted by our representative and transferred comfortably to your hotel for check-in.

In the evening, enjoy a memorable Dhow Cruise Dinner at Dubai Marina, sailing along the waterfront surrounded by spectacular skyscrapers and illuminated city views. Savour an international buffet dinner accompanied by live entertainment while experiencing the charm of Dubai by night.

Return to your hotel for an overnight stay.

Day 2
DUBAI CITY TOUR & BURJ KHALIFA

Overnight Stay: Dubai

After breakfast at the hotel, embark on a guided half-day Dubai city tour, exploring the city's iconic landmarks and cultural highlights.

Enjoy photo stops at the beautiful Jumeirah Mosque and the world-famous Burj Al Arab. Continue along the spectacular Palm Jumeirah, offering impressive views of Dubai's luxurious waterfront developments. Discover the traditional Gold Souk and Spice Souk, where the city's rich trading heritage comes alive.

Later, visit the magnificent Burj Khalifa, the world's tallest building, and ascend to the 124th-floor observation deck for breathtaking panoramic views of Dubai's skyline.Return to your hotel and enjoy the remainder of the day at leisure.

Day 3
DUBAI DESERT SAFARI & BBQ DINNER

Overnight Stay: Dubai

Enjoy breakfast at the hotel and spend the morning at leisure.

In the afternoon, prepare for an exciting Desert Safari Adventure in a 4x4 vehicle across the golden Arabian dunes. Experience thrilling dune bashing, capture memorable photographs of the desert landscape, and enjoy the spectacular sunset.

Continue to a traditional desert camp, where you can experience Arabian hospitality with Arabic coffee, dates, and cultural entertainment, including Tanoura dance and traditional belly dance performances, subject to availability.

Conclude the evening with a delicious BBQ dinner under the desert sky before returning to your hotel.

Day 4
THE VIEW AT THE PALM, MIRACLE GARDEN & GLOBAL VILLAGE

Overnight Stay: Dubai

After breakfast, visit The View at The Palm, an impressive observation deck offering panoramic views of Palm Jumeirah, the Arabian Gulf, and Dubai's spectacular skyline.

Later, explore the enchanting Dubai Miracle Garden, a colourful floral attraction featuring creative displays, flower-covered structures, and beautifully landscaped pathways. Enjoy leisure time taking photographs and discovering the garden's unique floral designs.

Continue to Global Village, a vibrant multicultural destination featuring international pavilions, traditional handicrafts, global cuisines, live performances, and exciting entertainment.

Enjoy the lively atmosphere before returning to your hotel.

Note: Miracle Garden and Global Village are seasonal attractions and operate subject to their announced opening dates.

Day 5
ABU DHABI CITY TOUR & YAS ISLAND

Overnight Stay: Dubai

After breakfast, depart for Abu Dhabi, the elegant capital of the United Arab Emirates.

Begin your sightseeing with a visit to the magnificent Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque, renowned for its grand white domes, intricate Islamic architecture, and beautiful decorative details. Continue along the scenic Abu Dhabi Corniche and enjoy photo stops near the iconic Emirates Palace, subject to access and traffic conditions.

Later, proceed to Yas Island, one of the UAE's leading entertainment destinations. Enjoy leisure time or choose an optional visit to one of its world-class attractions, such as Ferrari World Abu Dhabi, Warner Bros. World Abu Dhabi, or Yas Waterworld. Attraction admission tickets are subject to the selected package.

In the evening, return to Dubai for an overnight stay.

Important: Appropriate modest clothing is required when visiting Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque.

Day 6
DEPARTURE FROM DUBAI

Breakfast at Hotel | Airport Transfer

Enjoy your final breakfast at the hotel before checking out. At the scheduled time, transfer to Dubai International Airport for your onward flight.

Depart with wonderful memories of Dubai's spectacular skyline, luxury experiences, desert adventures, cultural attractions, and warm Arabian hospitality.

End of Tour — Thank You for Choosing Us!
